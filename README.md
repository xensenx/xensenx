# Introduction

<div align="center">
  <p>Hi, I'm Sen, or that's what I go by online. Now, calling me a full-fledged programmer would be a stretch. I code mostly using AI tools, also referred to as "vibe coding." But I'm not a stranger to coding either; I do know a thing or two about it, and I stay updated on what's happening in the tech world all around. Although staying updated for me usually involves watching a few YouTube videos and reading a couple of articles.

Anyhow, I do put effort into thinking things through before making tools. My focus is to make software that's practical, grounded, and something that actually solves real-world problems or removes small and annoying frictions in life. And here in this library of open source archives, I believe you'd find many such tools, so feel free to poke around!

So who am I? There is little I feel comfortable sharing about myself online... What I can talk about are things I'm passionate about. For example, I'm deeply fascinated with the technology of Artificial Intelligence. It seems like everybody these days is into it, doesn't it?

That may be true, but my fascination with AI stretches slightly past the current trends. I'd like to see it this way: AI is a life-changing technology. It's terrifying at the point where we are able to replicate and harvest our brain's cognitive powers into any digital medium through the means of data prediction and machine learning. And behind all that technical jargon, the applications of this technology are truly vast.

I'd like to believe we are in a transition period of something generational. As someone who saw the rise of this technology, it's interesting to see how and where it leads to. Even a glimpse of that future seems exciting. And what better way to go about it than to work in the forefront of this technology... or adjacent to it. Even small contributions in this field have the potential to compound exponentially in unexpected ways. Or so I believe.

In any case, aside from all the personal ideologies, I'm essentially an indie dev with an itch to solve problems and build software.</p>
