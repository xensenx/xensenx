About Me

Sen

I’m Sen — or, at least, that’s what I go by online. I’m your typical 19-year-old, learning and figuring things out as I go. My interests range from general technology to deep philosophy, and writing, building software tools, and creating designs are all ways for me to express those interests in different forms. The process itself can be quite therapeutic; I enjoy getting into that flow. What I enjoy even more, though, is seeing something meaningful emerge from it — something that earns its keep.

Outside of these pursuits, I’m a Computer Science student, with AI/ML as my main area of study. Speaking of AI, I’m personally fascinated by the technology. Seems like everybody is these days, doesn’t it? But I like to think my fascination stretches a little beyond the current trends. The way I see it, we’re in a transitional phase toward something major. When you consider the possibility of technology that can mimic aspects of the brain’s cognitive abilities, the possibilities become truly vast. The idea that AI could eventually become deeply integrated into everyday life, far beyond its current capabilities, is genuinely exciting to me. And what better way to explore that future than to work at the forefront of the technology, or even somewhere adjacent to it? I believe that even small contributions to this field have the potential to grow exponentially.

Outside of all that, I spend my free time in various ways — reading fiction and non-fiction, playing chess, and watching anime.

If you’d like to personally connect with me, whether for collaboration or any other reason, feel free to reach out through the contact section of this site.
